## II. Disetifikeiti tsa Botlhokwa Tse E leng gore Morekisi wa Dithulaganyo ya Alarama ya Bosenyi O Tshwanetse a di Tshwara

1. [ISO 9001](https://www.iso.org/iso-9001-quality-management.html) – Tsamaiso ya Boleng

Se ke seng: ISO 9001 e netefatsa gore tsamaiso ya boleng ya morekisi (QMS) e ikobela melao ya lefatshe e e tsepameng mo tsamaisong ya tsamaiso, go tokafatsa go nna go tswelang pele, le go kgotsofatsa bareki.

Goreng se le botlhokwa: Bakeng sa bareki ba go reka ka bongata, ISO 9001 e bontsha dikgwetlho tsa go dira tsa morekisi. Morekisi o o nang le ISO 9001 o bontsha ka tlwaelo:

* Diprotokolo tsa go leka tsamaiso e e ntseng e dirisa.

* Go tshepega go go tshepega mo dikgolaganyong tsa go dira le diphetolo tsa go tlhama.

* Dipaakanyo tsa go se nne le boleng bo bo kwa tlase le tswelelo ya go tshepega e e matla.

Ditlamorago tsa lefatshe: Dikgwebo tsa go reka di ka lebella boleng ba seelo bo bo ka lebegwang, ya botlhokwa bakeng sa diponagalo tsa lefelo le le kgolo mo go nna le go nna le yuniti e le e nngwe mo dikgolelong tsa bongata.

2. [CE Marking](https://ec.europa.eu/growth/single-market/ce-marking_en) – Go Tshwana ga Europe

Se ke seng: CE marking e bontsha gore seelo se ikobela dikgwetlho tsa bophelo bo botoka, tshireletso, le tikologo ya EU, go akaretsa go tshwana ga motlakase wa magneite le tshireletso ya motlakase o o kwa tlase.

Goreng se le botlhokwa: Seelo sa CE se tlhokega go reka tsamaiso ya alarama ya bosenyi mo Lefelo la Kgwebo la Europe (EEA).

Ditlamorago tsa lefatshe: Bareki ba ba lebaganang le marke ya EU ba tswela ka kgwetlho tsa dikgolelo tsa go romela le dikgwetlho tsa molato ka gore seelo sa CE-marked se fetile tlhokomelo ya kgolagano. Se le botlhokwa thata bakeng sa dipanele tsa alarama tse di dirilweng ka waelese le tse di senang waelese le diperiferala tse di tshwanang.

3. [UL Standards](https://www.ul.com/services/standards-certification) – Setifikeiti ya Tshireletso ya North America

Se ke seng: Ditekanyetso tsa Underwriters Laboratories (UL) ke tse di kgolo ka nako ya tshireletso ya motlakase le seelo sa alarama mo U.S. le Canada.

Ditekanyetso tsa UL tse di tshwanang di akaretsa:

* UL 681 – Tsamaiso ya alarama ya bosenyi.

* UL 827 – Ditirelo tsa alarama tsa setsi sa kago.

Goreng se le botlhokwa: Setifikeiti ya UL e fa tshepego ya go se nne le tshepego gore tsamaiso e ikobela dikgwetlho tse di thata tsa tshireletso ya motlakase le tswelelo.

Ditlamorago tsa lefatshe: Bareki ba go reka ka bongata mo North America ba rua molemo wa go fokotsa ditshenyegelo tsa inshorense le kgatelelo ya molao mo go diriseng dithulaganyo tsa UL-setifikeitsweng.

4. [FCC Certification](https://www.fcc.gov/engineering-technology/electromagnetic-compatibility-division/equipment-authorization) – Go Tshwana ga Motlakase wa Magneite (US)

Se ke seng: Federal Communications Commission (FCC) e laola dithulaganyo tsa radio frequency. Se se akaretsa ditlhokego tsa alarama ya bosenyi ya waelese le baamohedi.

Goreng se le botlhokwa: Go amogela ga FCC e netefatsa gore go dirisa alarama ya waelese ga go kgoreletse dikgolelo tse dingwe tsa botlhokwa le go ikobela mekgwa ya RF emissions.

Ditlamorago tsa lefatshe: Bareki ba ba dirisang dipolatfomo tsa alarama ya GSM/4G/Wi-Fi ya waelese mo North America ba rua tshepego gore disignalo di tla dira ka molao le ka tshepego ka ntle le go kgoreletsa go se direng.

5. [EN 50131](https://standards.iteh.ai/catalog/standards/clc/3f3d3e83-2b0a-4a5b-9a2a-9b3b5a0d4f6e/en-50131-1-2006) – Tekanyetso ya Go Tsenya le Go Tswa ga Alarama ya Europe

Se ke seng: EN 50131 ke tekanyetso ya alarama e e akaretsang mo Europe e e laolang dikarolo tsa grading (Grades 1–4), mekgwa ya go leka, le dikgwetlho tsa tswelelo.

Goreng se le botlhokwa: Grading e bua ka kotlololo mo go tshepega ga kgatelelo le tswelelo ya tshireletso.

Ditlamorago tsa lefatshe: Bakeng sa diponagalo tsa botlhokwa tsa tsamaiso—jo bo tshwanang le disenthara tsa data, diporopo tsa sefofane, kgotsa dikgolelo tsa madi—tsamaiso ya grading e e kwa godimo (m.m. Grade 3 kgotsa Grade 4) e fa bareki tshepego gore dithulaganyo di ikobela dikgwetlho tsa tsamaiso tse di thata.

6. Ditekanyetso tsa Tikologo – [RoHS](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/topics/waste-and-recycling/rohs-directive_en) & [REACH](https://echa.europa.eu/regulations/reach/understanding-reach)

Se ke seng:

* RoHS e thibela dilo tse di kotsi mo dithulaganyong tsa motlakase.

* REACH e laola dikhemikhale le go dirisa tsona ka tshireletso mo EU.

Goreng tsona di le botlhokwa: Go ikobela tikologo go tshwaranetswe le dikgwetlho tsa go reka tsa kgwebo e e tsepameng mo go tswelela.

Ditlamorago tsa lefatshe: Bareki ba ba tsepameng le dikgolelo tsa tikologo, setlhopha, le tsamaiso (ESG) ba rua molemo wa go dira mmogo le morekisi o o nang le dikarolo tsa morekisi le dikgwetlho tsa go dira tse di fokotsang dilo tse di kotsi.

7. [CCC](https://www.ccc-mark.com/) – Setifikeiti ya Go Tlhokega ga China

Se ke seng: CCC ke setifikeiti ya tshireletso le boleng ya botlhokwa ya China.

Goreng se le botlhokwa: Morekisi o o dirang mo China o tshwanetse a ikobela CCC go reka ka molao mo lefatsheng le ka nako e nngwe go bontsha go ikobela mo dikgolelong tsa Asia-Pacific tse di kgolo.

Ditlamorago tsa lefatshe: Ba reki ba lefatshe, go akaretsa ba ba reka bongata bo bo kgolo go tswa mo China, ba bona go ikobela ga CCC go thusa go tsamaya ka kgwetlho ya kgolagano ya Asia-Pacific.

## III. Diphetolo tsa Tikologo mo Ditekanyetso le Dikgwetlho tsa Go Ikobela

Europe

* Tikologo ya kgolagano di akaretsa CE, EN 50131, le melao ya go sireletsa data (m.m. [GDPR](https://gdpr.eu/)) mo tsamaiso ya alarama e dirisang netweke kgotsa ditirelo tsa leru.

* Ka morago ga Brexit, [UKCA marking](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/using-the-ukca-marking) e nna sebaka sa dikgwetlho tsa CE bakeng sa tsena mo markeng ya UK.

North America

* Ditekanyetso tsa UL le FCC di laola kgolagano ya botlhokwa, e e oketswang ke dikgwetlho tsa setheo sa kago/ tshireletso tsa setheo le tse di tswang mo lefatsheng.

* Bareki ba Canada ba tshwanetse ba netefatsa [ULC](https://canada.ul.com/ulcstandards/) (Underwriters Laboratories of Canada) tse di tshwanang.

Asia-Pacific

* CCC ya China le disetifikeiti tsa TELEC ya Japan di botlhokwa thata bakeng sa seelo mo tikologong e.

* Dikgolelo tsa go tswa jo bo tshwanang le India di ka tlhoka [BIS](https://bis.gov.in/) (Bureau of Indian Standards) kgotsa go ikobela go tshwanang.

Dikgwetlho tsa Go Tshwaranetsa Lefatshe

Dikgolelo tsa lefatshe jo bo tshwanang le [International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC)](https://www.iec.ch/) di tlhama ditekanyetso tse di tsepameng go laola mekgwa ya go leka, go fokotsa dikgwetlho tsa go leka tse di kgatlhang le go tsamaya ka kgwetlho ya kgolagano ya kgolagano.

## IV. Dikgwetlho le Ditshenyegelo tsa Go Se Ikobela

Go se ikobela ga se taba ya kgolagano fela; ke ya tsamaiso le ya madi:

* Dikgwetlho tsa molao: Diponelelo, go kgatlha go tswa, kgotsa go thibelwa mo dikgolelong tsa botlhokwa.

* Go kgoreletsa tsamaiso: Diphetolo tsa kgwebo ka lebaka la go tswa mo dikgolelong.

* Kgobo ya leina: Kgobo ya leina ya go nna le kgobo ya leina ya go nna le dikgolelo kgotsa go kgoreletsa ditekanyetso tsa lefelo.

Bakeng sa dikgwebo tsa bareki, go leka disetifikeiti go thibela dipaakanyo tsa go tokafatsa go nna le nako e telele ka morago ga go tsenya.
