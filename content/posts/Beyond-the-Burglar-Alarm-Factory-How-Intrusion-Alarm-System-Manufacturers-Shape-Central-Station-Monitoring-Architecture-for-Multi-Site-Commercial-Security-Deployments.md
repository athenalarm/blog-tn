## Puisano ya ditsela tse pedi ya go tsamaisa melaetsa, failover le event buffering mo phetisong ya alamo

Phetiso ya data ya maemo a tshoganyetso go tswa mo edge ya kgwebo go ya go seteishene sa bogare sa tlhokomelo ya alamo e tlhoka puisano ya ditsela tse pedi e e nonofileng. Diphanele tsa segompieno di kopanya ditsela tsa lebelo le le kwa godimo tsa TCP/IP (LAN) le puisano ya selefouno (GSM/4G LTE).

Firmware e tshwanetse go tshegetsa parallel socket connections tsotlhe ka nako e le nngwe. Mo boemong jwa go ikaega ka sequential failover fela—kwa tsela ya selefouno e simololang fela morago ga go latlhegelwa gotlhe ga LAN—puisano ya ditsela tse pedi e e nonofileng e tshegetsa ditsela tse di tshelang ka nako e le nngwe kgotsa e laola instant sub-second failover. 

Le fa go ntse jalo, go botlhokwa go lemoga fa failover ya tatelano e ka diegisa phetiso ya ditiragalo tsa botlhokwa fa tsela ya LAN e palelwa pele tsela ya selefouno e tsenngwa tirisong. Mo ditsamaisong tsa boenjenere tse di tlhabolotsweng, thulaganyo ya go tsamaisa melaetsa e laolwa ka ditsela tse di latelang:

1. Teko ya Tsela ya Ntlha: Netefatso ya phetiso ya pakete mo teng ga motsotswana o o tlhaotsweng. Fa e atlega, tshegetsa socket ya ntlha ya IP mme o tswelele ka routine heartbeat ya tlhokomelo ya kgokagano.
2. Lemogo la Phoso: Go latlhegelwa ga karabo maiing a moamogeli wa seteishene sa bogare sa tlhokomelo ya alamo wa ntlha. Romela data ka bofefo go bese ya alamo ya RS-485 ya phetiso ya firmware ya morago.
3. Tsenyo ya Tsela ya Selefouno: Tshekatsheko ya seemo sa carrier registration le thwalo ya bofefo jwa letshwao. Buffer local event logs mo non-volatile memory fa kgokagano ya selefouno e diegile.
4. Phetiso ya Tiragalo: Amogela cryptographic acknowledgment (ACK) pakete go tswa mo moamogeling wa morago. Tshegetsa phetiso ya selefouno go fitlha kgokagano ya LAN e tlhomama ka lobaka lo lo tlhaotsweng.

Durante a go palelwa ga netweke ya lefelo, phanele ya maemo a kgwebo e dirisa intelligent local event buffer e e bolokang dikete tsa chronological logs mo non-volatile memory. Fa kgokagano e boela, e dirisa auto-reconnection routine go resynchronize le CMS server, e ntsha dinalamo ka mokgwa wa first-in, first-out (FIFO).

## Technical FAQ

**Ke eng se se farologanyang motlhansi wa ditsamaiso tsa alamo ya tshireletso ya dikgwebo le feme e e tlwaelegileng ya hardware?** Feme e e tlwaelegileng e itebaganya fela le go kopanya hardware ya motheo jaaka dipolasetiki tsa sensor le dinalamo tsa analog tse di ikaegileng ka ditsela tsa mogala tsa legacy (PSTN). Motlhansi wa ditsamaiso tsa dikgwebo o ranya network-centric ecosystem yotlhe, go akaretsa edge computing hardware jaaka [phanele ya taolo ya alamo ya Athenalarm AS-9000](https://athenalarm.com/burglar-alarm/intrusion-alarm-panel/alarm-control-panel/), software ya tlhokomelo, le go tsenya protokholo ya SIA DC-09 ya phetiso ya ditiragalo ka IP bakeng sa kopanyo e e phephefeng le diteishene tsa bogare.

**Ke eng fa software ya tlhokomelo ya alamo e le botlhokwa go tshwana le hardware ya phanele ka boyone?** Software ya tlhokomelo ke yone e laolang go tsamaya ga data yotlhe, go patisanya melaetsa, le go netefatsa dinalamo mo server. Fa software e sa nonofa, hardware ya phanele ya taolo ya alamo ga e kake ya kgona go romela melaetsa ya yone ka botsitso go seteishene sa bogare sa tlhokomelo ya alamo.

**Ke meralo efe ya puisano e e nayang botsitso jo bo kwa godimo mo ditsamaisong tsa alamo tsa kgwebo?** Sebopego se se nayang botsitso jo bo kwa godimo ke puisano ya ditsela tse pedi e e patisitsweng e e kopanyang LAN (TCP/IP) jaaka tsela ya ntlha le 4G LTE cellular jaaka tsela ya morago, e reeletse matshwao a heartbeat ya tlhokomelo ya kgokagano a a tswelelang go supa bofefo jwa ditiro.

**Ka fa itirelang ga tlhokomelo ya seteishene sa bogare go amang ka gona nako ya tsiboso ya dinalamo?** Fa protokholo e tsisa data e e sekasekileng ka mokgwa o o bulegileng mme e tsenya netefatso ya alamo ka video, operator o bona tshedimosetso yotlhe ka motsotswana mme o kgona go romela babetli ba molao ka bofefo ntle le go tsaya nako a fetolela dikhoutu tsa hex tse di sa tlhalosegeng.

**Ke eng fa go tsenngwa ga mafelo a le mantsi go tlhoka meralo e e farologaneng le ya lefelo le le lengwe fela?** Mafelo a le mantsi a kgwebo (jaaka dibanka kgotsa ditsela tsa mabenkele) a tlhoka centralized management architecture e e letlang go deploy templates tsa kgaolo, go laola firmware lifecycle ya kgakala, le go kgobokanya dinalamo tsa mafelo otlhe ka thwalo mo setsing se le sengwe sa SOC ntle le go romela batlalehi mo tshimong.

**Ke eng se mofani wa dinalamo a tshwanetseng go se sekaseka pele a tlhaola OEM burglar alarm manufacturer?** Mofani o tshwanetse go batla motlhansi yo o tshegetsang protokholo ya SIA DC-09 ya phetiso ya ditiragalo ka IP e e bulegileng, modular hardware expansion, ditsela tsa tlhophiso ya dilingwa tsa kgaolo, le ditifikeiti tsa boditšhabatšhaba jaaka ISO9001 le IEC 62368-1.

**Ka fa dinalamo tsa TCP/IP di tlhabololang ka gona scalability ya ditsamaiso tsotlhe?** D dinalamo tsa TCP/IP di tlosa dithibelo tsa megala ya mogala ya setso ka go dirisa virtualized network sockets mo godimo ga server, e e letlang go laola dikete tsa diphanele ka nako e le nngwe ntle le ditshenyegelo tse di kwa godimo tsa kago ya megala ya diatla.

**Karolo efe e netefatso ya alamo ka video e e tshamekang mo go fokotseng dinalamo tsa maaka?** E golaganya trigger ya sensor le media clip ya nako ya nnete, e e letlang operator mo go seteishene sa bogare sa tlhokomelo ya alamo go bona thwalo gore a go na le thunyo ya nnete kgotsa ke phoso ya tikologo, ka jalo e fokotsa ditshenyegelo tsa dispatch ya maaka.

**Puisano ya ditsela tse pedi ya alamo ke eng, mme e tsenngwa tirisong jang?** Ke go gokaganya phanele le ditsela di le pedi tse di ikemetseng tsa netweke (LAN + 4G LTE). Tsamaiso e romela routine heartbeat ya tlhokomelo ya kgokagano mo tseleng ya ntlha, mme firmware e fetolela data yotlhe ka bofefo go tsela ya morago fa e le gore kgokagano ya ntlha e wetse kgotsa e kgaotse.

**A seteishene sa bogare sa tlhokomelo se ka kgona go laola dikete tsa diphanele ka nako e le nngwe?** Ee, fa e le gore se dirisa scalable infrastructure e e nang le ditsamaiso tsa SQL database, parallel packet processing, le software e e nonofileng jaaka Alarm Center Management suite ya Athenalarm e e sefang lerata la matshwao a diagnostic mme e tlogela fela dinalamo tsa maemo a kwa godimo.

**Ka fa bese ya alamo ya RS-485 e tshwarang ka gona megala e meleele mo meahong e megolo?** E dirisa differential signaling mo godimo ga twisted pair cable e e nang le tshireletso kgatlhanong le EMI, e e lekanyetsang pharologanyo ya motlakase (V_A - V_B) go thibela lerata la motlakase o mogolo, mme e tlhoka 120-ohm termination resistors kwa bofelong ba mola go thibela packet reflection.

**End-of-line (EOL) resistors ke eng, mme ke eng fa ditsamaiso tsa kgwebo di di tlhoka?** Ke ditsela tsa motlakase tse di tsenngwang kwa bofelong jwa mogala wa zone loop go baka baseline resistance e phanele ya taolo ya alamo e e lekolang nako yotlhe, e e letlang phanele go farologanya zone secure, alamo, short-circuit, kgotsa go kgaolwa ga megala ka boomo.

**Protokholo ya SIA DC-09 ke eng, mme ke eng fa e kgethwa go feta mekgwa ya poraefete?** SIA DC-09 ke kaelo e e bulegileng ya boditšhabatšhaba e e tlhalosang ka fa data ya alamo, account details, le dinkhoutu tsa ditiragalo di phuthelwang ka gona mo dipaketeng tsa TCP/IP, e netefatsa gore phanele e ka buisana le moamogeli mongwe le mongwe wa third-party CMS ntle le vendor lock-in.

**Ka fa ditsamaiso tsa alamo tsa kgwebo di fokotsang ka gona dinalamo tsa maaka tsa tikologo?** Di dirisa ditsela tsa intelligent pulse counting, cross-zone verification (e e tlhokang gore di-sensor tse pedi tse di ikemetseng di thunye pele ga alamo), le ditsela tsa diagnostics tsa firmware tse di sekasekang ditiragalo tsotlhe kgatlhanong le histori ya ditiro tsa lefelo.

**Ke dikgato dife tse di tlhokegang go laola remote firmware updates ka tshireletso mo diphaneleng tsa kgwebo?** Thulaganyo e tsamaya ka secure session, e romela firmware file mme e netefatsa integrity ka cryptographic checksum, e lekola gore phanele e mo seemong sa disarmed le go nna le backup battery e e tletseng, mme e tsenya bootloader routine e e kgonang go dira rollback ka boyone fa go ka nna le kgaolo ya motlakase nako ya tlhabololo.
